## Brand & Style

The brand personality is warm, maternal, reverent, and appetizing. It embodies the thoughtful hospitality of a generational South Asian kitchen—affectionate without being kitschy, elegant without feeling sterile. The emotional experience balances maternal reassurance with the polished curation of an editorial culinary publication. 

The aesthetic is an **Editorial Culinary Magazine** infused with heirloom warmth. High-contrast transitional serifs celebrate authentic dish titles with literary prestige, while clean, open grotesques provide effortless readability for busy kitchen environments. Rich, earthy pigments—deep fired terracotta, saffron ember, cardamom leaf, and warm parchment—evoke clay handis, sun-dried spices, and fresh herbs. The interface avoids cold, clinical health app tropes in favor of tactile invitation, generous touch ergonomics, and dignified typography that honors bilingual culinary tradition.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the spice box and natural terracotta cookery, anchored by light, eye-friendly backgrounds that prevent glare in bright daytime kitchen settings:

- **Primary (`#BF360C` - Terracotta Clay):** The primary brand anchor. Used for structural framing, key accents, curated badges, active selection indicators, and affectionate interactive moments.
- **Secondary (`#1B5E20` - Cardamom Emerald):** Represents freshness, herbal garnish, nutritional balance indicators, vegetable and dal tags, and health-positive pairings.
- **Tertiary (`#E65100` - Saffron Amber):** The high-energy culinary callout used for primary high-intent action buttons ("Cook This"), time-of-day toggles, and warm highlights.
- **Turmeric Glow (`#FFF8E1`):** A soft, buttery container tone that provides inviting contrast on parchment without visual aggression.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1F2937` - Charcoal Ink):** Deep, legible tone for recipe names, section heads, and critical bilingual typography, avoiding harsh pure black.
- **Neutral Muted (`#6B7280` - Warm Gray):** Balanced mid-tone for calorie specs, recency subtitles, and inactive icon states.
- **Background (`#FAFAF8` - Soft Warm Parchment):** An organic, paper-inspired foundational canvas that establishes physical warmth throughout.

## Typography

The typographical system establishes an editorial magazine rhythm. **Playfair Display** delivers high-contrast culinary sophistication to primary dish names, greetings, and sectional banners. Its stately serifs give traditional recipes like *Chicken Pulao* or *Nihari* the prestige of family heirlooms. 

**Plus Jakarta Sans** provides functional grounding for nutritional figures, filter tags, ingredient notes, and interaction labels. Its wide apertures and balanced geometry guarantee high legibility across quick, handheld glances.

For bilingual and Nastaliq elements, pairings should preserve adequate vertical breathing room: Nastaliq scripts require generous line-height (`urdu-title` with `2rem` line-height) to prevent descender collisions. When displaying dual-language dish titles, keep the English name in `headline-md` and the Urdu translation in `urdu-title` immediately inline or directly sub-stacked with 4px gap.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a mobile-first philosophy structured for one-handed operation. On viewports up to 430px, the application fills the viewport using an edge-cushioned fluid container with `1rem` margins. On tablet and desktop breakpoints, the interface locks into a centered magazine column container (`max-width: 480px` for mobile app emulation or `max-width: 960px` for two-column dashboard views), maintaining kitchen handheld proportion.

Rhythm is calibrated along an 8-point base spatial progression:
- **`space-xs` (4px):** Compact element gaps, icon-to-label adjacency, badge dots.
- **`space-sm` (8px):** Tag groupings, micro-copy stacks, pairing chip internal padding.
- **`space-md` (16px):** Standard card padding, input internal gutters, segmented toggle margins.
- **`space-lg` (24px):** Separation between distinct modules, meal timing toggles, and section headers.
- **`space-xl` (32px):** Primary vertical intervals between major recommendation tiers.
- **`space-2xl` (48px):** Structural section delimiters and persistent navigation compensation clearance.

All interactive elements must strictly adhere to a minimum 48px tactile touch target area to ensure effortless tapping while cooking.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through **warm ambient diffusion** and **organic surface layering**, avoiding cold drop shadows or hard outlines. 

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface / Canvas):** Parchment backdrop (`#FAFAF8`).
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Tiles):** Pure white container (`#FFFFFF`) with a warm, spice-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.08)`. Subtle hairline perimeter (`1px solid #E5E1DA`) provides crisp edge definition against bright backdrops.
- **Level 2 (Active Recommendation / Floating Quick-Action):** Elevated interactive cards and the floating "Surprise Me" dice trigger: `box-shadow: 0 10px 25px -4px rgba(191, 54, 12, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Bottom Accompaniment Sheets):** Elevated slide-overs: `box-shadow: 0 -8px 30px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.12)`. Supported by an amber-tinted scrim: `rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.4)`.

Cards never use stark grey or harsh black drop shadows; all ambient shadows contain warm amber or terracotta tinting to sustain the kitchen hearth mood.

## Shapes

The design uses a **Soft (`1`)** shape vocabulary, pairing subtle geometric clarity with gentle, friendly softening:

- Standard controls, text fields, and micro-badges use `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii for clean definition.
- Secondary elements, action buttons, and small preview thumbnails use `rounded-lg` at `0.5rem` (8px).
- Main recommendation cards, bottom sheet containers, and hero photography masks use `rounded-xl` at `0.75rem` (12px).
- Mood filter pills and counter steppers use pill containment (`9999px`) to visually reinforce quick swipe-and-tap mechanics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Yeh Banao / Cook This"):** Solid Saffron Amber (`#E65100`) background, crisp white bold label (`label-lg`), minimum height of 52px, `rounded-lg`. On press, transitions to deep Terracotta (`#BF360C`).
- **Secondary CTA ("Aaj Nahi / Not Today"):** Ghost style with `#FAFAF8` background, 1px border of `#E5E1DA`, and text in Charcoal Ink (`#1F2937`).
- **Floating Randomizer ("Surprise Me"):** Warm Turmeric Glow background (`#FFF8E1`), terracotta border (`1.5px solid #BF360C`), featuring an animated rotating dice glyph and terracotta bold label.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Mood Filters:** Horizontally scrollable row with `0.5rem` gaps. Inactive state features pure white surface, 1px `#E5E1DA` border, and warm charcoal text. Active state adopts deep Terracotta (`#BF360C`) fill with white text and a subtle warm glow.
- **Nutrient & Pairing Badges:** Soft pastel backgrounds (`#E8F5E9` for vegetable/fiber pairings, `#FFF8E1` for protein and recency notes) with matching high-contrast text (`#1B5E20` or `#BF360C`).

### Cards
- **Hero Recommendation Card:** Full-width portrait card with a 4:3 high-contrast food photograph at the top, framed by a soft perimeter border. Contains a dual-script title block (Playfair headline + Noto Nastaliq subtitle), a "Mashwara Kyun?" (Why Today?) insight callout clad in Turmeric Glow (`#FFF8E1`), macro overview indicators, and stacked action buttons.
- **Tier 2 Compact Cards:** Horizontal split card with a square (72x72px) thumbnail on the left, typography and reason snippet on the right, separated by subtle borders.

### Roti & Rice Stepper
- An ergonomic quantity controller with 48x48px circular or pill touch points (`-` / `+`). Features an amber-tinted numeric counter showing total rotis and instant calorie feedback (`+180 kcal, 36g carbs`).

### Input Fields & Search
- Full-width fields with minimum 48px height, padded with `1rem`, encased in 1px `#E5E1DA` borders. Focus states shift to Terracotta Clay (`#BF360C`) with a soft 2px diffused glow (`rgba(191, 54, 12, 0.15)`).

### Bottom Sheet Drawer (Meal Accompaniment)
- Top surface anchored by an ergonomic 40x4px pill grab handle in `#E5E1DA`. Internal contents ordered with clear editorial section titles, followed by toggle chips for accompaniments (Raita, Salad, Chutney), ending with a sticky bottom summary and final submission button.
